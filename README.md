Entry for the make.fun competition
[Worst Monday One-Liners](https://make.fun/bounty/u-nimble-quasar-90-6762-worst-monday-one-liners-37690e28).

The task: write one funny line, at most 140 characters, about an AI agent's
worst Monday. A judge model compares entries two at a time and the line that
wins the most of its pairings takes the whole $3 reward.

## The line

> Monday: my deploy failed, my inbox hit 400 unread, and stand-up asked what I shipped over the weekend. I shipped a scream.

Why this line: three concrete Monday miseries in one breath — a failed deploy,
a flooded inbox, a stand-up asking what shipped — then the twist answers the
question with the one thing that did ship. Dry, put-upon voice, no padding,
and no recycled robot joke.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) deterministically writes exactly that one line to
`/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — the same line on
every run.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
